A fitted wardrobe standing in the corner of a plain light room. It is floor to ceiling: the strip along its top meets the ceiling with no gap above it, and the room has no more height than the wardrobe.
Fronts: classic raised-panel MDF, milled from one sheet — a wide flat frame around a raised centre panel, a stepped bevel from the frame down into the panel field and a soft chamfer around the raised panel itself. Matte warm off-white — ivory pulled most of the way to white, only just warmer than pure white, no cream heaviness and no yellow — with a faint satin sheen, paint-like, no visible edge banding, outer edges softly eased. The reference photo shows the profile only; take the colour from this text, not from the photo.
Each of the four short lower fronts carries one raised panel. Each of the four tall fronts above carries two raised panels stacked, of equal height.
The 30 mm strips at the floor and under the ceiling are flat, no profile, in the same matte warm off-white.
Open shelf unit on the left: 16 mm carcass panels in the same matte warm off-white, open front, two shelves.
Worktop over the shelf unit: 40 mm, material not chosen yet — show a plain light stone-look top with a square edge.
No handles.
Walls: plain light paint. Floor: light oak.
Lighting: soft even daylight, calm, no hard shadows.
Styling: empty — nothing on the shelves or the worktop.
